# Trophy photograph sources

The game displays a separate photographic cutout of the actual trophy for each of its 18 domestic leagues and three European competitions. A World Cup image is also included in the catalogue. Images are stored locally, so the game does not depend on third-party image requests.

The downloaded PNG files are unchanged 100 ? 100 images from Transfermarkt’s achievement pages. They replace the generic SVG cup in the career museum and season ceremony, and the generated cup previously used by the UCL bracket and on-pitch ceremony. These source images are credited to Transfermarkt and their respective rights holders; this document does not assert a new license.
