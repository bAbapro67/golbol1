# User-supplied kit references

The active player and goalkeeper shirts are hand-drawn Canvas graphics based on the 24 photos supplied by the user on 2026-09-30. The game does not load or paste these shirt photos. Local copies live in assets/kit-references; original Downloads files are unchanged. Indices 0-11: Barcelona, Real Madrid, Manchester City, PSG, Galatasaray, Fenerbahce, Besiktas, Milan, Bayern Munich, Liverpool, Inter Miami, Al Nassr. Each index has a -player.jpg and -keeper.jpg.

Patterns, sponsor lettering, brand marks and collars are drawn with paths, lines and text. Existing club crest assets are used as small badges. Sleeves use matching colours, geometric details and the pictured sleeve length. Shorts and socks are matching game interpretations because the references show shirts. Inter Miami uses the supplied black shirt. Preview: artifacts/kit-preview.html.
